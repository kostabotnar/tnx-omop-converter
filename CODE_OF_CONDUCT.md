# Code of conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, by reference: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

All participants in issues, discussions, pull requests, and other project spaces are expected to follow it. In short: be respectful, accept constructive feedback, and do not harass or discriminate.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, Kostiantyn Botnar, at kosta.botnar@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately and in good faith. The maintainer follows the enforcement guidelines in the Covenant.
